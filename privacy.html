Privacy Policy: Gear Garden

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Gear Garden does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

  • The app has no accounts, sign-in or profiles.
  • Your game progress (stars, best times and best move counts) and whether you have seen the tutorial are saved only on your own device. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • The app makes no network requests and works fully offline. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.

If a future update ever changes this (for example, an optional online leaderboard), this page will be updated first and the change explained here in plain language.
