Gear Garden Support

Gear Garden is a calm, offline gear puzzle game for iPhone. Tap a gear to turn it and every gear meshed with it, and solve each puzzle by getting every marker pointing up.

Contact

For help, bug reports or feedback, email DialInnStudio26@protonmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

Do I need an account or internet connection? No. Gear Garden works fully offline and has no accounts.

Where is my progress saved? Stars, best times and best move counts are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Are there ads or in-app purchases? No.
